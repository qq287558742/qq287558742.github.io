About these notes

This site publishes short sourcing notes for independent retailers. Each note answers one question: which SKUs in a category come back, and why.

The underlying data is aggregate order history across 65,000 consumer SKUs, scored for demand and competition. It is order data rather than vendor marketing copy, and we do not publish figures we cannot support.

Written by Jason Zhou, Operations Lead at DPJ Wholesale, a B2B wholesale catalog. Product catalog and wholesale pricing: dpjwholesale.com.
