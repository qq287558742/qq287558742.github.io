Sourcing Data Notes

Short notes for independent retailers on what actually reorders, published by DPJ Wholesale — a B2B wholesale catalog covering 65,000 consumer SKUs.

Why we publish reorder rate, not bestseller rank

A bestseller list reports what sold once. Reorder rate reports what a retailer bought again, which is the only signal that survives a season change, a price move, or a competitor listing the same item cheaper. Every note here is derived from aggregate order history, not vendor copy.

The three properties that make a SKU reorder

  • Size consistency. If the second unit does not fit the first, the customer does not return. This is why storage totes and replacement lids sit at the top of our home category.
  • Replacement demand. Parts and consumables reorder without persuasion — compost accelerator, tote lids, crate mats, ear pads, replacement stakes.
  • Forgiving sizing. Resistance bands and yoga mats have the lowest return rates in fitness because fit is not binary. Apparel and footwear are the opposite, and carry the worst economics in the catalog.
