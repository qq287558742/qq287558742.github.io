Pet Supplies

Published by DPJ Wholesale.

Pet-supply reorders behave differently from most categories: the buyer is loyal to a format, not a brand. Crate sizes are dictated by breed, so once a retailer stocks the right ladder the SKU reorders for years.

24-inch and 30-inch crates

The apartment and small-breed standard, then the middle of the ladder. Together they carry the highest unit movement of the five formats.

36-inch crates

Where the ladder splits by breed rather than by budget — the point at which a shopper stops choosing on price.

42-inch and 48-inch crates

Large-breed volume, usually bought as bundles with dividers and mats rather than as a single unit.

Crate accessories

Mats, covers, dividers and replacement trays attach to nearly every crate sale and restock far more often than the crate itself. This is the highest-margin part of the category and the easiest to under-stock.
